FinCorp Inc. — Annual Financial Report 2024
This report summarises the financial performance of FinCorp Inc. for the fiscal year ending 31 December
2024. The company operates across three primary business segments: enterprise software licensing,
professional services, and cloud infrastructure. Total revenue grew by 47% year-over-year, driven primarily by
strong demand in the cloud infrastructure segment and successful expansion into Southeast Asian markets.
Net profit margin improved from 18% in FY 2023 to 23% in FY 2024, reflecting disciplined cost management
and improved operational efficiency across all business units. The board has approved a dividend of $0.45
per share for Q4 2024, to be distributed on 15 February 2025.
Key risks identified for FY 2025 include macroeconomic uncertainty in emerging markets, increased
competition in the cloud infrastructure space, and potential regulatory changes affecting data residency
requirements in the European Union.

Segment Performance Breakdown
FinCorp operates three reportable segments. Enterprise Software Licensing contributed 38% of total revenue,
with annual recurring revenue (ARR) reaching $3.2M, up from $2.1M in FY 2023. The Professional Services
segment delivered $1.9M in revenue, constrained by talent availability in the second half of the year. Cloud
Infrastructure was the standout performer, growing 112% to $3.4M as three large enterprise clients migrated
workloads to FinCorp-managed cloud environments.

Outlook and Forward Guidance
Management projects total revenue of $12.5M to $13.5M for FY 2025, representing growth of 47% to 59%
over FY 2024. This guidance assumes stable macroeconomic conditions and successful closure of three
large enterprise pipeline deals currently in late-stage negotiation. Capital expenditure is expected to increase
to $1.8M as the company expands its cloud infrastructure capacity to support anticipated demand.
The board remains committed to maintaining a minimum cash reserve of $2.0M and will evaluate additional
dividend distributions on a quarterly basis, subject to operating cash flow performance.
